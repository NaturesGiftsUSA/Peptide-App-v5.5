PEPTIDE PROTOCOL // TRACKER — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Peptide Protocol Tracker is a free, personal tool for logging protocols, doses, inventory and notes. It is designed so that your data stays yours.

What the app stores

The protocols, log entries, inventory and notes you enter. Nothing else.

Where your data is kept

  • Without signing in: your data is saved only in your own web browser on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • If you sign in with Google: your data is also saved as a single file in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The app runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to Google; there is no separate server.

What we can see

Nothing. The app's owner has no access to your browser storage or your Google Drive, and no data is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.

Google permissions

  • Google Drive app data — lets the app read and write only its own private file. It cannot see, change or delete any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address — used only to show which account is connected.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Tap Options → Disconnect in the app to stop syncing.
  • To delete the copy in Google Drive, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Peptide Protocol Tracker → Delete hidden app data.
  • To delete the copy in your browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

You can also revoke the app's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
